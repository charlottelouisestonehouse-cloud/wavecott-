Location & Local Guide

Everything Croyde Has to Offer — on Your Doorstep

Croyde sits at the heart of one of the most beautiful stretches of the North Devon coast. From world-class surf beaches to dramatic headland walks and hidden village pubs.

12 min walk

Croyde Beach

One of the UK's best surf beaches — fast, powerful waves best suited to intermediate surfers. Beginners will love nearby Saunton Sands.

35–40 min walk

Baggy Point

A spectacular National Trust headland walk with panoramic views across Croyde Bay. One of North Devon's finest coastal walks.

2 min walk

Croyde Village

Pubs, cafes, surf shops and restaurants — including The Thatch, Blue Groove, and Billy Budd's — all within a short stroll.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How far is Wavecott from Croyde beach?

Wavecott is approximately a 12-minute walk from Croyde beach, and just 150 metres from the centre of Croyde village with its cafes, pubs and surf shops.

Is there parking at Wavecott?

Yes — there is a designated parking space on the private drive at the front of the property, included with your stay at no extra charge.

Can I book without using Airbnb?

Absolutely — we encourage direct bookings. You'll pay the same nightly rate with no platform service fees added. Get in touch via the contact form or call us directly.

Is Wavecott suitable for surfers?

Yes — Croyde is one of the UK's best surf destinations. We have a surfboard guests are welcome to borrow, and several surf schools operate locally.

Is the property fully self-contained?

Yes — Wavecott has its own private entrance, patio, kitchen and bathroom. You won't share any spaces with other guests or the owners.

What is the minimum stay at Wavecott?

The minimum stay is 2 nights. Check-in is from 4pm and check-out is by 11am. A non-refundable deposit is required to secure your booking.
